Our Story

Because every celebration is a unique moment for you and your loved ones, we do everything we can to make it a success. A premier caterer in Rabat, Salé and Témara, Dar Rbati brings you its craftsmanship, experience and logistical expertise to bring your finest receptions to life.

  • Listening to your expectations during a personalised meeting.
  • A detailed proposal following that consultation.
  • Attentive follow-up throughout your event.

Personalisation

Your event is one of a kind. We help you design and imagine the reception that reflects you — true to Moroccan tradition, with a modern touch.

  • Several service tiers with menus signed by our Chef.
  • A selection of culinary animations to amaze your guests.
  • Floral ambiance and full styling of the venue.
  • Bespoke table art: from simplicity to pure refinement.

Dar Rbati and its team will craft a service worthy of your event, and a selection of refined menus to delight every one of your guests.
